# DH Robotics PGI-140-80 CAD asset record

## Required variant

`PGI-140-80-W-S-M1-L5-J1-F1-01`

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `PGI-140-80` | PGI body, 40-140 N per jaw, 80 mm total stroke |
| `W` | Brake fitted |
| `S` | Side cable exit |
| `M1` | Modbus RTU (RS485) + Digital I/O (NN) |
| `L5` | 5 m cable |
| `J1` | Standard fingertips |
| `F1` | Standard flange |
| `01` | UR e-Series/compatible robot cable option in catalog EN.2403 |

The A801-0056-WG debug cable is not required for simulation.

## Official evidence

Retrieval attempt date: **2026-08-25 (Asia/Hong_Kong)**.

1. [DH Robotics PGIA/PGI product page](https://en.dh-robotics.com/product/pgia)
   lists the PGI/PGIA family, an 80 mm stroke, 1 kg body, and current CAD
   downloads. The page currently labels the family `PGIA` and shows a
   97 x 62 x 86 mm envelope and ±0.02 mm repeatability, versus the legacy PGI
   catalog's 95 x 61.7 x 86 mm and ±0.03 mm. It is not proof that PGIA geometry
   is identical to the requested legacy PGI configuration.
2. [Official DH Robotics Servo Electric Gripper Catalog EN.2403](https://en.dh-robotics.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Servo-Electric-Gripper-Catalog_DH-Robotics_EN.2403.pdf),
   pages 56-57, documents the PGI selection code, J1, F1, robot-cable `01`,
   40-140 N per jaw, 80 mm stroke, 1.1 s opening/closing, ±0.03 mm repeatability,
   1 kg body mass excluding fingers, 95 x 61.7 x 86 mm envelope, and the F1
   ISO 9409-1-50-4-M6 pattern.

## Required manual retrieval and verification

1. Open the official product page and request/download the exact PGI-140-80
   body plus shared J1 fingertip and F1 flange assets from DH Robotics.
2. Ask DH Robotics to confirm in writing whether the current PGIA archive is
   dimensionally and mechanically identical to legacy PGI for this exact
   `W-S-M1-L5-J1-F1-01` configuration.
3. Save raw files under the ignored path:

   ```text
   assets/vendor/dh_robotics/pgi_140_80/
   ```

4. Record actual names and checksums without renaming the originals:

   ```bash
   sha256sum assets/vendor/dh_robotics/pgi_140_80/*
   ```

5. Inspect the assembly in a CAD tool and record:
   - native units and import scale;
   - assembly origin and axis directions;
   - F1 mating face, pilot, PCD, bolt holes, and tool-normal direction;
   - J1 closed position, maximum opening, and whether 80 mm means 40 mm per
     jaw;
   - cable-exit side and brake-body envelope;
   - part masses, centers of gravity, and inertias when supplied.
6. Compare the F1 face to ISO 9409-1-50-4-M6 and the UR10e tool flange.
7. Obtain explicit CAD redistribution/derivative-mesh permission before
   committing any detailed vendor geometry.

## Units and coordinate convention

The official engineering drawing dimensions are millimetres. The project
Xacro uses metres. The CAD archive's native units, origin, handedness, and
opening axis are **pending** because the archive is not available locally.
No final orientation or mesh transform may be inferred from a product render.

## Repository policy

Raw CAD is intentionally uncommitted and ignored. Redistribution terms were
not found on the official download page, so detailed converted meshes must also
remain uncommitted until permission is clear. The current Xacro contains only
project-created primitive placeholders derived from published envelope data.
